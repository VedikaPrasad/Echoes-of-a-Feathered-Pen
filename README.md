## 5. My Mom

> MY MOM
> 
> My mother is at seventy eight,
> Still she is very strict,
> She is for me a gift,
> I preserve it without a rift,
> 
> Yet she hasn't become oblivion,
> Neither an irregular civilian.
> Never deviated from her routine,
> She'll ever remain the chatelaine.
> 
> Her day starts even before seven.
> She treats this abode a heaven.
> Watching all the serials is her hobby
> None can interfere beyond the lobby.
> 
> At times I scare to stare
> Especially when she dozes on easy chair.
> She looks like a corpse
> Which I wish to get it lapse.
> 
> She has her own plus and minus
> However in my life she is a bonus.
> I wish to keep her at ease
> In whatever may be the case.
> 
> We are mere players in this universe,
> And old age is a sheer curse.
> I pray thee God!
> Never ever make me too old.

## 7. Walkathan

> WALKATHAN
> Complete lock has compelled
> The whole world to a stand still!
> It has made the life helter-skelter
> And a great havoc caused the life hell!
> The pathetic plight of the Labour...
> Who seek solace and shelter
> Are on the march of walkathan..
> They're well aware.....
> East or West home is the best!
> Even the crafty corona would wonder
> To see their guts and willpower!
> The languid labourers lament
> And opine that before the touch of corona
> Hunger would munch them like hyena!
> Let's extend our helping hands
> Before they really die of hunger!

>

> Unaware that it was just temporary!
> When the demons devise a plan
> The Deity delegates its clan.
> 
> He ensures that the truth reveals
> Whenever on the earth's evil prevails!
> To prove good over evil
> He comes back again and again from the hail!
